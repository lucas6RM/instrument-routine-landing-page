_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

Instrument Routine is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

## Data stored on your device

Your routine, exercises, practice history, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive or have access to this data. It may be included in device backups managed by Apple, depending on your settings.

## Purchases

Optional Coffee Tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

## Analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies. This website also uses no analytics cookies.

## External links

Links you add to exercises open outside Instrument Routine. The privacy practices of those websites are governed by their respective operators.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version and its effective date will remain available on this page.
